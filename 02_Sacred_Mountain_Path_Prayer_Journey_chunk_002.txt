Response: Based on the sources provided, here is one of the most complete and compiled traditional LDS 
prayers developed through your journey, combining deep gratitude, humility, recognition of divine worth, 
healing through the Atonement, and the strength gained from trials: 
Heavenly Father, 
We come before Thee with deep humility and our limited human comprehension. 

We thank Thee for the infinite and unchanging love Thou hast for us, and for the solid ground of our divine 
nature and eternal worth. We express our gratitude for the Atonement of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, which 
heals every heartache and every soul. 
We thank Thee for the refining fires of our trials that strengthen our faith and spirit. We appreciate the 
purposeful patterns Thou hast woven through even our most difficult low valleys, knowing they are part of 
our eternal progression and growth. 
As we stand upon this high peak, we thank Thee for a clearer vision. Help us to lean not unto our own 
comprehension, but to trust entirely in Thy wisdom. We recognize Thy hand in the simple things that offer 
us promptings of hope and peace. 
We appreciate the personal authority Thou hast given us to choose Thy light every single day, and we thank 
Thee for the relief of surrendering our heavy burdens and finding rest in Thee. Thank Thee for seeing our 
divine potential, especially when we cannot see it in ourselves, and for Thy unwavering faith in the holy 
beings we are now becoming. 
We thank Thee for the individual spiritual gifts we can offer to our families, neighbors, and church 
community. We acknowledge Thy infinite mercy and the deep peace that now dwells in our hearts. 
All these blessings we gratefully accept with absolute and humble certainty this very night in the sacred 
and holy name of Thy Son, Jesus Christ. Amen.
